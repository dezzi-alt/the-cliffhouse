Seven rooms. Three you can browse here.

Every room at The Cliffhouse faces Walker Bay. These three are our most requested — the rest are shown on request when you enquire.

The Whale Room

R3,450 / night

Our closest room to the deck, with a private step-out balcony and the widest uninterrupted view of the bay. During whale season, guests here rarely close the curtains.

  • King bed, sea-facing
  • Private step-out balcony
  • Underfloor heating
  • Solar-heated en-suite
  • Nespresso & fynbos tea
  • Fibre Wi-Fi

The Fynbos Suite

R4,100 / night

The largest room in the house, with a separate sitting area and a private terrace planted with indigenous fynbos. Guests who stay a week choose this room first.

  • King bed + sitting area
  • Private fynbos terrace
  • Freestanding bath
  • Underfloor heating
  • Nespresso & fynbos tea
  • Fibre Wi-Fi

The Harbour View

R2,950 / night

A quieter room at the end of the passage, angled toward the Old Harbour lights, one row back from the open bay. Our best value room, and the favourite of returning guests.

  • Queen bed, harbour-facing
  • Reading nook
  • Solar-heated en-suite
  • Underfloor heating
  • Nespresso & fynbos tea
  • Fibre Wi-Fi
