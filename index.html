Our Story

A fisherman's cottage, reborn on the cliff edge.

The Cliffhouse began as a single stone cottage on the Hermanus cliff path, restored room by room until it became seven. We kept the bones — thick walls, small-paned windows, a view that hasn't changed in a century — and added the rest: linen the colour of sand, water heated by the sun, and a deck built for nothing but watching the bay. Every room faces the water. Every stay includes the walk.
